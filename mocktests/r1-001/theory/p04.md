$$
A=\begin{pmatrix}
-7&2\\
5&-4\\
1&8

$$

$$
\lVert A\rVert_F=\sqrt{\frac{p}{q}},
$$


where $p$ and $q$ are positive integers and $\gcd(p,q)=1$.

$$

$$
