## Part 2.1 (5 points)

Reasoning is not required.
Coding is not allowed.

Let

\[
f(x)=\tanh(4x-7).
\]

At a point \(x_0\), it is known that

\[
\tanh(4x_0-7)=\frac{5}{13}.
\]

Compute \(f'(x_0)\).
Write the result in the unique form \(p/q\), where \(p\) and \(q\) are integers, \(q>0\), and \(\gcd(|p|,q)=1\).
What is \(p+q\)?

- **A.** \(45\)
- **B.** \(313\)
- **C.** \(745\)
- **D.** \(945\)
- **E.** \(2473\)

## Part 2.2 (5 points)

Reasoning is not required.
Coding is not allowed.

The random variables \(X\), \(Y\), and \(T\) are mutually independent and have mean \(0\), with

\[
\operatorname{Var}[X]=\frac{7}{3},\qquad
\operatorname{Var}[Y]=\frac{5}{2},\qquad
\operatorname{Var}[T]=\frac{11}{6}.
\]

For

\[
z=2X-3Y+\frac12T,
\]

state the exact value of \(\operatorname{Var}[z]\).

## Part 2.3 (5 points)

Reasoning is required.
Coding is not allowed.

A pre-activation is

\[
z=\sum_{k=1}^{169} W_k x_k.
\]

The inputs \(x_1,\ldots,x_{169}\) are mutually independent, each with mean \(0\) and variance \(1\).
The weights \(W_1,\ldots,W_{169}\) are mutually independent, each with mean \(0\) and standard deviation \(\sigma>0\), and all weights are independent of all inputs.
The bias is \(0\).

Derive the exact value of \(\sigma\) for which \(\operatorname{Var}[z]=1\).
