## Scope and curricular boundary

The canonical owner is the existing Book 2 roadmap row `B2-023-generative-models-diffusion`.
The unit teaches adversarial and diffusion generative modelling, then shows how Stable Diffusion composes B2-022's autoencoder, B2-021's U-Net, B2-019/B2-020's attention and token embeddings, and a diffusion scheduler into a latent, text-conditioned pipeline.

**Stable Diffusion boundary.** The `stable-diffusion` concept is taught as an architecture and a pipeline: latent diffusion, text conditioning by cross-attention, classifier-free guidance, and the scheduler.
It is practised through faithful CPU-scale analogues that a student builds and trains from scratch.
Loading real Stable Diffusion weights needs a GPU and a model hub, so it is out of scope here; the GPU capstone (B2-024) owns that workflow.

GAN networks are multilayer perceptrons.
Transposed-convolution (DCGAN) generators appear only as a forward-only "going deeper" topic, because Book 1 never teaches transposed convolution.

All inputs, targets, splits, seeds, and probes are literal or generated by committed, seeded source code.
Training is CPU-only and fixed-seed, and every fresh solution notebook completes within 20 seconds.
No web download, torchvision dataset, pretrained model, opaque checkpoint, or student data is allowed.
No solution needs a sandbox; there is no anti-cheat machinery (user directive, 2026-08-28).

### Taught in this unit (not in the baseline, Book 1, or earlier Book 2 units)

- **Binary cross-entropy with logits (Session 1).** Taught as the two-class special case of `book1:cross-entropy-loss`, with the sigmoid written out and `torch.nn.functional.binary_cross_entropy_with_logits` as the stable API.
- **Jensen–Shannon divergence (Session 1).** Defined from B2-022's KL divergence through the mixture `M = (P+Q)/2`. Nonnegativity, with equality iff `P = Q`, follows from B2-022's `KL ≥ 0`.
- **Pointwise optimization of an integral objective (Session 1).** For the optimal discriminator: maximizing `a·log y + b·log(1−y)` over `y ∈ (0,1)` for each point separately, using single-variable calculus.
- **Sum of independent Gaussians (Session 3).** `N(0, s²) + N(0, r²)` independent is `N(0, s² + r²)`. It is stated with a variance check from `book1:variance`, and used to collapse the forward process.
- **Gaussian posterior of the forward process (Session 4).** The formula for `q(x_{t−1} | x_t, x_0)`, its mean `μ̃_t` and variance `β̃_t`, is stated, not derived. The unit derives only the consequence: KL between two equal-variance Gaussians is a scaled squared mean difference, which becomes the ε-prediction objective (from B2-022's Gaussian KL).
- **Classifier-free guidance (Session 5).** `ε̂ = ε_uncond + w·(ε_cond − ε_uncond)` with conditioning dropout during training.
- **Cross-attention conditioning (Session 5).** Image/latent tokens are queries; text tokens are keys and values. This is B2-019's Q/K/V with B2-021's modal-ownership rule.

|---:|---|---|
| 1 | `01-adversarial-games.ipynb` | generator and discriminator roles; BCE-with-logits; minimax value function; optimal discriminator (pointwise derivation); JS divergence and the global optimum; non-saturating generator loss and why it fixes early vanishing gradients |
| 2 | `02-training-and-diagnosing-gans.ipynb` | alternating updates with `detach` discipline and separate optimizers; loss traces versus sample quality; mode collapse and mode-coverage diagnostics on a known mixture; discriminator overpowering; a fixed-seed tiny 2-D GAN training trace |

| 4 | `04-denoising-and-sampling.ipynb` | reverse process; stated posterior mean and variance; equal-variance Gaussian KL leading to the ε-prediction loss; the simplified training objective with random `t`; the ancestral sampling loop; a fixed-seed tiny 2-D DDPM training and sampling trace |
| 5 | `05-latent-and-conditional-diffusion.ipynb` | why diffuse in a latent space (B2-022 autoencoder), the latent scaling factor, text conditioning by cross-attention, conditioning dropout and classifier-free guidance, the full Stable Diffusion pipeline component map (text encoder, U-Net denoiser, VAE decoder, scheduler), and a CPU-scale class-conditional latent diffusion trace |
